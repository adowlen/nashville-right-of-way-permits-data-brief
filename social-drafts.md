## LinkedIn draft (for a future Friday synthesis selection)

80.6% of the scheduled window-days in Nashville's right-of-way register are excavations.

Of 20,500 records in Metro's Active Right-of-Way Permits layer, lane closures are the majority by count (11,132, median window 6 days). Excavations are 6,348 records - but at a median window of about 90 days, they hold 563,765 of 699,124 scheduled window-days.

Only 2,102 records (10.3%) have a window that overlaps Nov 12, 2026. And the layer's Onstreet, Council District, and ZIP fields are populated on 6 of 20,500 records - the 6 legacy records - so district-level accounting cannot be done from the layer as published.
